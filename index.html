CALibrate A Pokémon Crystal save tool

Make CAL someone else.

In Crystal, the Trainer House battle against CAL uses the team and name of whoever you last used Mystery Gift with. Load two Crystal saves and CALibrate writes each player's team into the other's game.

How it works

  1. Reads the other save's player name and party the same way the game does during a Mystery Gift link: level, species and four moves per Pokémon, eggs skipped.
  2. Writes that into the Mystery Gift block of your save, which is where the Trainer House reads CAL's team and name.
  3. Sets the Trainer House flag, so CAL shows up even if you never linked.

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded. The page only changes 51 bytes, and the save's checksum doesn't cover them, so nothing else in the file moves.
